CUSHIONMAN vs RED HULK
======================
A 1986-style arcade adventure. Soft but mighty!

The rampaging RED HULK has stolen every pillow in Pillow City. Fight your way
through his pillow-thief henchmen across the night-time city streets, then
take him on in a rooftop showdown and put him down for a nap.

HOW TO START
------------
Double-click index.html. It opens in Chrome or any modern browser.
It works offline, with no install and no internet connection needed.
Press ENTER or SPACE on the title screen to start.
(Sound starts after your first key press. That's how browsers handle audio.)

CONTROLS
--------
  Move ................ Arrow keys or A / D
  Jump ................ Space, Up arrow, or W
  Float ............... HOLD jump while falling (your cape works like a parachute)
  Cushion Punch ....... Z or J
  Throw a Cushion ..... X or K   (up to 3 on screen at a time)
  CUSHION SLAM ........ In the air, hold Down (or S) and press Z/J
                        You crash down and send out a feather shockwave
  Pause ............... P or Esc
  Mute / unmute ....... M
  Start / continue .... Enter

THE LEVEL
---------
Pillow City streets. Watch out for:
  * Pillow Thieves: striped burglars carrying sacks of stolen pillows. They walk at you.
  * Brick Tossers: orange vests. They lob bricks in an arc. Punch or throw a
    cushion at a brick to knock it out of the air.
  * Moths: a cushion's natural enemy! They flutter down to snack on you.
Bounce on the pink SOFAS to launch high into the air. Collect gold buttons for points and
hearts for health. Touch the pillow flag halfway along to set a checkpoint.

THE BOSS: RED HULK
------------------
Every attack is announced first with a flashing "!", so watch him closely:
  * CHARGE: he crouches and stamps, and red dashes show his path. Then he
    barrels across the roof. Jump over him or get up on a girder. If he hits
    the wall he is stunned for a moment. Pile in with punches!
  * GROUND SLAM: he raises both fists and the floor glows. Shockwaves then run
    along the ground both ways. Jump over them.
  * LEAPING SMASH: he crouches and a red target marks where he will land.
    Get out of the way!
  * At half health he gets ANGRY. He is faster, he does double slams, rocks
    fall from the sky (watch for their shadows) and he can chain attacks.
    A heart falls from the sky to help you.
He's tired after each attack. That's your best chance to hit him, but every
hit counts at any time: watch for the white flash and the "POW!" numbers.
Walking into him doesn't hurt (he just nudges you back). Only his CHARGE, the
landing of his LEAPING SMASH, the shockwaves and the falling rocks do damage.
The girders keep you safe from his slam, charge and leap, but from up there
you can only reach him with thrown cushions.
Punch = 4 damage, thrown cushion = 2, Cushion Slam = 6 (he has 70 HP).

You have 5 hearts and 3 lives. If you lose all your lives, press ENTER to
continue from the checkpoint or from the boss fight (your score resets).

Have fun, and sweet dreams, Pillow City!
